Rorschach Playing Cards

November 2020 – Visual Identity & Print Design

Rorschach Playing Cards is a deck designed for general use while having a strong visual which can be used for magic tricks, cardistry, or regular play. The design is inspired by the famous Rorschach inkblot test used in psychological interpretation.
