Mateo J. Villalba-Rodriguez

Graphic designer (and photographer) based in Brooklyn, working in branding, print and publication design, with an interest in arts and culture. At NYU Gallatin I studied Opening Up Perspectives in Visual Design Practices: how design can move past assumptions and adapt to different people and environments. I care most about how design is experienced, and about making work that feels considered to the people who come across it. Currently designing for NYU’s Center for Multicultural Education and Programs, alongside independent clients.
